When launching a product requires millions in upfront engineering and compliance costs, insurers only build for massive corporate clients. They cannot afford to experiment.

## Core Agentic Suite

### 1. Market Benchmarking Agent

* Scans multi-region competitor filings, claims trends, and economic indicators across key hubs (Nairobi, Lagos, Johannesburg, Cairo, Abidjan).
* Identifies coverage gaps and auto-benchmarks premium rates against local market standards in real time.

### 2. Rate-Plan & Actuarial Agent

* Generates risk matrices, pricing engines, and loss-ratio simulations tailored to hyper-local risk profiles.
* Executes mathematical models and complex spreadsheets inside isolated, zero-trust sandboxes—ensuring 100% deterministic accuracy for underwriting teams without compromising underlying data.

### 3. Regulatory & Filing Agent

* Drafts complete regulatory compliance packages formatted strictly to the requirements of local authorities (e.g., IRA in Kenya, NAICOM in Nigeria, CIMA across Francophone West/Central Africa).
* Translates legal and financial parameters into local regulatory structures instantly.

### 4. Lifecycle & Compliance Monitor

* Runs background monitoring agents 24/7 in stateful, ephemeral environments.
* Detects rate drifts, emerging regulatory mandates, or shifting loss ratios, alerting compliance officers before compliance breaches occur.
